How MOTM Enabled Pipeline Visibility Beyond Referrals for an EPC Company

A case study demonstrating how structured sales execution transformed a referral-dependent business into a proactive, process-driven sales organization in India's environmental services sector.

50+ Active leads in 6 months

Client snapshot

Business
A specialized EPC firm focused on turnkey execution of Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP) and Sewage Treatment Plants (STP) across industrial and municipal sectors in India
Business type
ETP / STP turnkey execution with full project lifecycle management
Sales model
Traditionally referral-driven with strong reliance on existing networks
Sales cycle
Extended timelines typical of capital-intensive infrastructure projects

Starting point: the challenge

Before engaging MOTM, the company faced significant constraints in their sales approach. Growth was limited by the unpredictability of referral-based business development, with no systematic outbound activity to generate new opportunities.

  • New business source: 100% dependent on referrals from existing clients and industry contacts, creating unpredictable revenue streams
  • Outbound activity: Zero proactive prospecting initiatives—no cold calling, email campaigns, or systematic lead generation
  • Follow-up discipline: Inconsistent prospect engagement with opportunities falling through cracks due to lack of structured process
  • Pipeline visibility: Limited insight into deal stages, conversion rates, or forecasting accuracy for business planning
  • Internal bandwidth: Technical and operational teams stretched thin with no dedicated sales execution resources

Why MOTM was engaged

The leadership team recognized that sustainable growth required a fundamental shift from reactive referral dependence to proactive sales execution. They needed a partner who could establish professional sales discipline without requiring significant internal resource investment.

  • Outreach beyond referrals: Systematically identify and engage qualified prospects across target industries and geographies
  • Consistent follow-ups: Implement disciplined cadence to nurture relationships and advance opportunities through the pipeline
  • Professional execution support: Access experienced sales resources without the overhead of full-time hiring and training

The MOTM solution

MOTM implemented a comprehensive sales execution framework designed specifically for the long sales cycles and relationship-driven nature of the EPC environmental services market. The solution combined account-based strategy, content-led engagement, and systematic process discipline to drive consistent outreach and conversion.

This kind of outreach required extensive engagement with multiple decision-makers within each organization to effectively communicate value and move opportunities forward. To address this, MOTM deployed an Account-Based Marketing (ABM) strategy, where content-led engagement was used to educate and influence key decision-makers, supported by omni-channel connectivity across phone calls, emails, messaging, and selective field interactions.

  • Calling, email & omni-channel follow-ups: Coordinated outreach across phone, email, messaging, and field visits to ensure consistent, personalized engagement with all relevant stakeholders
  • Prospect tracking & pipeline management: CRM-based pipeline management capturing every interaction, follow-up, and stage progression across extended sales cycles
  • Regular reporting & performance visibility: Weekly performance dashboards providing leadership with clear visibility into outreach activity, pipeline movement, and conversion health
  • Execution accountability: Defined KPIs, follow-up standards, and quality benchmarks ensuring disciplined execution across all active opportunities

Execution output: measurable activity

Within the first six months of engagement, MOTM generated over 50 active leads and converted 1 turnkey project—demonstrating the immediate impact of systematic outbound execution. The company experienced unprecedented visibility into their market reach and opportunity development process.

50+Active leads generatedConversations initiated with more than 50 qualified prospects within 6 months, across target segments previously unreachable through referral networks alone
  • Turnkey project converted: Successfully closed a major turnkey ETP/STP project, demonstrating the effectiveness of systematic follow-up and pipeline management
  • Sales activity visibility: Clear reporting dashboards providing leadership with real-time pipeline insights and forecasting data
  • Follow-up consistency: High-frequency engagement maintained with all active opportunities throughout extended sales cycles

Business impact

  • Market visibility increased: Company presence established in new industrial segments and geographic regions beyond existing referral network reach
  • Pipeline clarity improved: Leadership gained accurate forecasting ability with stage-by-stage visibility into deal progression and conversion probabilities
  • Sales discipline established: Systematic processes embedded across the organization, creating sustainable foundation for continued growth

The transformation extended beyond metrics to fundamentally change how the organization approached business development. Sales became a managed, predictable function rather than an opportunistic hope.
